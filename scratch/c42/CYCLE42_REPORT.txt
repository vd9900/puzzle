5. HONEST CONCLUSION
   - No progress toward the prize. Three new families are closed: the
     dbbi-style a1z26 prime construction on faed; ±k / 2k / k/2 of
     chain-derived keys against the prize key; constant-digit windows to
     1e8. Two more were closed by critic checks: faed in the puzzle's own
     objects, and linear recurrences.
   - 15 of the 21 ideas were dead before testing: repeats of exhausted
     families, guess families or conditional tooling. The supply of truly
     new ideas is thinning.
   - Every view of faed so far (global statistics, linear complexity, prime
     streams, a1z26 tokens, constants, the puzzle's own objects) matches
     i.i.d. uniform decimal digits written a-i, with g standing for 0 or 7.
     The most likely reading is that faed is keyed or ciphertext-like
     output. Solving it would need a key or derivation we do not have yet,
     most plausibly from the unsolved inner/small blobs or an outside hint.
     More self-describing decodes of faed are unlikely to pay off.
   - Worth adopting as tooling: patch bw_oracle to check x-coordinates
     (k, n-k, 2k, k/2 for free; not done yet), and record the size fact
     (plaintext 64-79 bytes, a 64-hex string fits, a bare WIF does not).
   - Open, low priority: T0 with a zero-based counter and reversed-digit
     tokens; the pending gamma 1e8 scan.
