KEY FACTS
 - Phase-3 riddle quote source = BMCB ch.1 'A Design for the Future' opening ("the future is fluid ... ours to direct"). The riddle line "the ...... is ours" (6 dots)
   was never part of the Phase-3 password; fillers 'future' (quote) and 'choice' (TVP film 'The Choice Is Ours', anti-Merovingian logic) both fit.
 - 'salvation' appears exactly once in each of BMCB and DtF, same intro: "We are our own salvation or damnation" -- only Fresco text with a SalPhaseIon(=salvation)+duality co-occurrence.
   Not in LF or 1995 RoC. Wikiquote lists it (DtF p.10).
 - No 'matrix', 'last words', 'yin/yang', 'in front of your eyes', '140'/'hundred forty', 'theory of everything' in any of the 4 texts. LF '140' only in two footnote page cites.
 - Fresco (Korzybski-style) explicitly argues against two-valued thinking (BMCB: "good and bad ... cause and effects"; LF: "polar opposites") -- thematic only.
 - Fresco died 2017-05-18 (Roxanne Meadows open letter); no documented 'last words'. "Don't build me a monument" is a legacy line, not deathbed.
 - 'in front of your eyes but you're not seeing it' is from the creator's 2023-02-23 binary roadmap, not from Fresco; 'Looking Forward' link is a solver pun; creator's reply was evasive (Cartman joke).
 - 2026-01-01 'Make the best of everything' is self-labelled "no hint" (decoded binary).
 - 'wise man above': Phase-3 'thinker' (earlier in text) and/or deceased by 2019 ('above'). 'worth hundred fourty of the investment' has no textual anchor in Fresco works;
   community readings (140 chars, RSA-140, 2*2*5*7) unsupported. Most consistent reading: ideological value statement (creator 2024: friends building communities around Jacque's ideas).
