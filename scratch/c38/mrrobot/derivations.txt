T1 hidd3nlynx

  instruction sheet (on screen, reconstructed by u/Metal_Monkey42) lists "HSM admin server creds frank.bowman:hidd3nlynx".
  It is the only password string shown in the episode that Phase 2 did not consume, and it literally says "hidden".
T2 frank.bowman:hidd3nlynx
  Same line of the sheet verbatim (oracle normalisation also gives frankbowmanhidd3nlynx).
T3 hidd3nlynx022350
  "where daughters hit magic keypads": Angela (Phillip Price's daughter) authenticates twice on screen: server password, then
  the Remote PED PIN 022350 typed on the PED keypad ("Please attend to the PED"). Puzzle grammar = concatenate answers in order.
T4 marlinspikemoxie
  The only spoken passphrase in the episode (Irving/Angela phone call-and-response, nod to Moxie Marlinspike); untested.
